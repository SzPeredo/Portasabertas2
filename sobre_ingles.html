Software Developer

Technology, programming and innovation

The professional and their importance

The professional is responsible for creating, developing, testing and maintaining programs, applications, websites and digital systems.

To perform this work, they use programming languages and different tools to transform ideas and needs into technological solutions.

In addition to creating new systems, professionals can also fix problems, improve existing programs and help maintain the security and functionality of applications.

Importability

A large part of the services used today depends on software. Banks, schools, hospitals, companies, social networks, stores and games use digital systems to carry out their activities.

Job market

Software development is present in several sectors of the economy. Professionals in this field can work in technology companies, banks, retail, industry, education, healthcare, entertainment and many other sectors.

Compensation can vary based on factors such as experience, specialization, region, company, and type of employment. Therefore, there is no fixed salary for developers, but the range is R$ 6,000 to R$ 8,300 per month.
